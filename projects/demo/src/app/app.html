Curved cinema, from a Kerusi document

Built from a KerusiMap + KerusiState and fed through adaptKerusiMap. Rows arc around the screen with each seat angled to face it; the screen and exits are non-bookable elements; aisles are simply absent seats. Booked, held and blocked seats are all distinct and unselectable.
